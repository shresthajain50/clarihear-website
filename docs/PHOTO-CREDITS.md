## Notes and caveats
- **Not verified:** whether the photographers hold model releases. Neither Unsplash nor Pexels guarantees releases for people or property. Faces are clearly visible in `conversation.jpg`, `lecture-or-tv.jpg`, `place-office.jpg` and `hero.jpg` (man at the table). Use them only as generic scenes, never in endorsement contexts.
- `place-office.jpg`: at full size you can just make out small brand marks on the monitors and desk phones, plus a tiny "I ♥ London" sticker on a cabinet. None of them is prominent. Blur or crop them if a close crop is used.
- `listening.jpg`: the earbud has the recognisable AirPods Pro shape (Pexels titles it that way), but no logo is visible. `earbuds.jpg`: the case and phone show no visible logos.
- `hero.jpg`: the real restaurant is not named on the source page, and there is no readable branding. Framed photos on the back wall appear at small size.
- `place-cafe.jpg`: bottle labels and Japanese menu cards are present but not legible as brands.
- I rejected these candidates: Unsplash+ items; AirPods and AUKEY/Huawei-branded earbud shots; a Midtown street with a jewellery billboard; a busy Palo Alto café with visible shop branding and prominent faces; a Pexels café with an ambiguous-age background figure; and TV scenes showing Netflix, a film poster or TV-brand logos.
