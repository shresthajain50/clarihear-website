What ClariHear is, and isn't

What it is

  • A sound amplifier app for everyday listening
  • Personalised with a quick in-app tone check
  • Built for adults 18 and over

What it isn't

  • Not a medical device
  • Doesn't diagnose or treat hearing loss
  • Not a replacement for a hearing professional
